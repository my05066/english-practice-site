> I'd like to talk about a skill I learned at university — writing code. I started from scratch, so at first everything looked difficult. I learned it step by step: I watched short lessons, tried small examples, and practised a little every day. After a while, I could build a small project on my own, and it actually worked. That moment made me really happy. In the end, I learned more than just code. I learned how to keep going when something is hard, and that is useful for my studies and for my future work.
